# ThunderClap Revenue OS

An outside-in revenue operating system built as part of my application for the Head of Sales role at ThunderClap.

The central question:

> How do you turn strong demand into predictable revenue without making founder bandwidth the bottleneck?

ThunderClap has publicly described a business where:

- revenue grew from approximately $600K in Year 1 to $1.1M in Year 2;
- the current ambition is approximately $3M;
- BANT-qualified inbound pipeline increased from approximately $300K to $550K;
- average ticket size increased from approximately $22K to $34K;
- account revenue contribution increased from approximately 18% to 30%;
- new-deal revenue growth has not kept pace with pipeline growth;
- founders continue to carry a meaningful share of discovery, proposals, follow-ups and closing.

That suggests a specific operating challenge:

**Demand exists. Revenue throughput needs to catch up.**

This repository explores how I would begin building the system around that problem.
